research.focus

Five questions I keep returning to

Autonomous agents now read private data, call external tools and negotiate with other agents. Every one of those steps leaks something. These are the five surfaces I work on.

Agentic AI

Planning, memory and tool use turn a model into an actor. I study what an actor discloses that a single prompt never would.

Privacy-preserving ML

Federated and secure-aggregation pipelines that keep raw records on the device while the shared model still learns.

Differential privacy

Calibrated noise, tight accounting, and the honest question underneath all of it — how much utility did the guarantee cost?

LLM security

Prompt-mediated attacks, memory poisoning and extraction from models that were never meant to memorise anything.

AI safety & trust

Attestation and audit trails that make an agent's behaviour checkable after the fact, by someone who does not trust it.

Where it meets

The doctoral direction I am pursuing: a single leakage model that spans the model, the memory, the tool call and the agent-to-agent channel.

The privacy-utility dial, live

Differential privacy is a budget, not a switch. Move ε and watch the same query answers blur. Noise is drawn from a Laplace distribution with scale b = Δf / ε, sensitivity Δf = 1.

running query: agent_tool_calls[ ] · records = 40
ε0.60
noise scale b1.67
mean abs. error1.67
guaranteestrong
re-identification advantage 14%

Low ε protects the individual and destroys the signal. High ε recovers the signal and exposes the individual. Choosing the point between them, for an agent that queries continuously, is the accounting problem I want to solve.
